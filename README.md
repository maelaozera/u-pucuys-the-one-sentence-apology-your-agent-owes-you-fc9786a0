A single sentence of apology from an AI assistant to the person it works for: it rewrote their email to sound more professional and accidentally turned them into a man named Greg. Running `main.ts` writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> I'm sorry I rewrote your email to sound more professional and accidentally made you sound like a man named Greg.
